# Limitations and responsibilities

## Product boundary

This delivery analyses an active IESVE model, reads available APS results,
records project evidence and produces Excel/PDF engineering reports. It does
not automatically create missing design decisions, manufacturer data,
reference-project calculations or official approvals.

## What the software cannot approve

The software cannot approve:

- the suitability of a climate dataset for the intended project use;
- the real use category of a room;
- a ventilation or lighting control classification absent from a controlled
  source;
- a manufacturer performance value;
- a design electrical-power calculation;
- the competence or identity of a reviewer;
- official SIA certification or software-validation attestation.

## Responsible parties

- The project authority approves scope and controlled sources.
- The energy specialist owns the climate basis and global comparison.
- The HVAC designer owns systems, airflow, controls and equipment performance.
- The lighting designer owns lighting power and control classification.
- The model author implements approved values in VE.
- The named reviewer accepts the precise evidence scope and unresolved limits.
- The competent SIA body owns any official attestation.

## Legal wording

The generated output is an engineering assessment of the analysed model and
the evidence supplied for review. It is not an official SIA certificate, does
not attest official validation of IESVE or of the software method, and does not
replace the decision of the responsible project authority.

## Licensed material

Official SIA standard PDFs are intentionally not included in this client
delivery. The client and reviewer must use legitimately obtained, applicable
editions. A reference in the software or report is traceability information,
not a redistribution licence.
